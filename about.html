Our Story
Built to Deliver Differently
Northramp began in 2012, when leaders who had come up through the largest consulting firms set out to deliver federal technology services differently — on a simple idea: technology should make missions stronger, not more complicated. They brought engineers and advisors together under one roof and built the firm intentionally: close to clients, close to the work.
That idea still drives us. We turn complexity into progress — pairing deep technical expertise with mission insight, applying Agile and Lean practices that scale, and holding ourselves to the highest federal standards. Innovation is something we deliver, from cloud and automation to AI. Not something we talk about.
Being a smaller firm is the point, not a caveat. Our senior people stay hands-on in your delivery, decisions happen in days rather than quarters, and the working relationship is closer than what the large firms offer. Every engagement carries our reputation — so we don't have a B team.
What truly sets Northramp apart is our people. Leadership stays accessible, ideas are shared freely, and contributions are recognized.

True North
True north
A compass does not tell you where to go. It tells you whether you are still headed there. That is the role we play on a program — we hold the bearing on the result you set out to reach, we make the trade-offs visible when the route changes, and we tell you plainly when something is not working. We are not here to fill seats or extend a contract. We are here to get you where you meant to go.

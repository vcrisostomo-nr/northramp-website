Our Story
Built to deliver differently
Northramp began in 2012, when leaders who had grown up in some of the largest consulting firms set out to deliver federal technology services differently. The idea was simple: technology should make missions stronger, not more complicated. They brought engineers and advisors together under one roof and built the firm intentionally, close to clients and close to the work.
That idea still drives us. We turn complexity into progress by pairing deep technical expertise with mission insight, applying Agile and Lean practices that scale, and working within the standards and constraints federal environments demand. Innovation is something we put to work, from cloud and automation to AI. Not something we simply talk about.
Being a smaller firm is the point, not a caveat. Our senior people stay hands-on, decisions move quickly, and the working relationship stays close. Every engagement carries our reputation, so there is no B team. in days rather than quarters, and the working relationship is closer than what the large firms offer. What truly sets Northramp apart is our people. Leadership stays accessible, ideas are shared freely, and contributions are recognized.

True North
We stay focused on where you need to go
A compass does not tell you where to go. It tells you whether you are still headed in the right direction. That is how we approach every engagement. We keep our bearing on the outcome you set out to achieve, make tradeoffs visible when conditions change, and tell you plainly when something is not working. We are not here to fill seats, extend a contract, or deliver a roadmap and walk away. We are here to help you reach the outcome that mattered in the first place.
